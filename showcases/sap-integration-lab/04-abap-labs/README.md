# ABAP Cloud Historical Labs — Engineering Evidence

[Versión en español](./README.es.md)

> **Scope:** curated public evidence from historical personal ABAP Cloud lab submissions across Curso 1's full 16-unit curriculum.  
> **Evidence boundary:** the 10 hands-on labs retain documented historical execution evidence, including the account owner's own sanitized screenshots. This track makes no current-runtime claim.

This track complements the broader [SAP Engineering Evidence Lab](../README.md). It does not replace the portfolio-wide [SAP Capability Matrix](../00-profile/SAP_CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md).

## Published evidence

- **10 ABAP class source files**, one per hands-on lab, across 8 base topics: types, arithmetic, conversions, text/string processing, control structures, internal tables and field symbols.
- **16/16 Curso 1 units accounted for**: 10 with a personal hands-on lab (source + the account owner's own sanitized screenshots), 6 theory-only units with no Laboratorio exercise — see the [lab catalog](./labs/README.md) for the full breakdown.
- **Historical execution evidence:** 10/10 documented in the original personal submissions and represented publicly by 20 sanitized owned screenshots.
- **5 training-derived guides:** ABAP Dictionary/DDIC, abapGit, ATC, ABAP Cleaner and authorizations.
- **4 Course 3 hands-on practices**, owner-confirmed and normalized to `_fq` source — see [Training Practices](./training-practices/README.md).
- **Bilingual documentation:** English + Spanish.

## Current evidence boundaries

- **CDS, RAP, Behavior Definitions, Service Definitions/Bindings, OData, and ABAP Unit:** no hands-on object of these types is represented in this historical/course-practice track, and none is claimed here.
- **abapGit, ATC, ABAP Cleaner, Authorizations:** hands-on evidence exists via [Course 3 Training Practices](./training-practices/README.md) (`OWNER_CONFIRMED_HANDS_ON_EXECUTION`), separate from the 10 historical labs.
- **Course 2 (ABAP Dictionary):** hands-on training occurred, but no saved source artifact exists to publish — see [Training Practices — Evidence Matrix](./training-practices/EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md).
- **Historical course-specific dependencies:** the original submitted sources remain unchanged where they reference objects such as `ZEMP_LOGALI` or `TEXT-001`; those references are part of the preserved historical source context and are not presented as current-runtime evidence.

A status such as `NOT_EVIDENCED_IN_THIS_LAB_SET` (see [Evidence Matrix](./EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md)) is local to this track. The broader portfolio separately contains documentation-grounded RAP architecture, source-reviewed ABAP Unit design patterns and an executable non-live OData client-side example.

Document metadata supports provenance under Francisco Quinteros' Office profile for all 10 hands-on labs, but is not treated as independent proof of every embedded element. Raw course files (Documentación/Laboratorio/Solución/Test PDFs) are not republished. ## Publication policy

This track excludes raw course files, certificate PDFs, QR/credential identifiers and private audit records. Published screenshots are limited to the account owner's own embedded captures, sanitized to remove account identifiers, tenant hostnames, technical usernames and training-environment data. The scope is complete on documented historical execution evidence and owner-confirmed hands-on training.
