# ABAP Cloud Historical Labs — Engineering Evidence

[Versión en español](./README.es.md)

> **Scope:** curated public evidence from historical personal ABAP Cloud lab submissions across Curso 1's full 16-unit curriculum.  
> **Evidence boundary:** the 10 hands-on labs retain their documented historical execution evidence, including the account owner's own sanitized screenshots. No current-runtime re-execution is claimed or required for this portfolio track.

This track complements the broader [SAP Engineering Evidence Lab](../README.md). It does not replace the portfolio-wide [SAP Capability Matrix](../00-profile/SAP_CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md).

## Published evidence

- **10 ABAP class source files**, one per hands-on lab, across 8 base topics: types, arithmetic, conversions, text/string processing, control structures, internal tables and field symbols.
- **16/16 Curso 1 units accounted for**: 10 with a personal hands-on lab (source + the account owner's own sanitized screenshots), 6 theory-only units with no Laboratorio exercise — see the [lab catalog](./labs/README.md) for the full breakdown.
- **Historical execution evidence:** 10/10 documented in private source submissions and shown via the account owner's own sanitized screenshots, published per lab.
- **Current-runtime claim:** none — this track is closed on documented historical evidence; see [Runtime-Readiness Reference](./runtime-readiness/README.md) for optional, non-blocking reproducibility material.
- **5 training-derived guides:** ABAP Dictionary/DDIC, abapGit, ATC, ABAP Cleaner and authorizations.
- **4 Course 3 hands-on practices**, owner-confirmed and normalized to `_fq` source — see [Training Practices](./training-practices/README.md).
- **Bilingual documentation:** English + Spanish.

## Current evidence boundaries

- **CDS, RAP, Behavior Definitions, Service Definitions/Bindings, OData, and ABAP Unit:** not present anywhere in the historical labs, Course 2, or the Course 3 practices — no such object exists in this track, and none is claimed.
- **abapGit, ATC, ABAP Cleaner, Authorizations:** hands-on evidence exists via [Course 3 Training Practices](./training-practices/README.md) (`OWNER_CONFIRMED_HANDS_ON_EXECUTION`), separate from the 10 historical labs above.
- **Course 2 (ABAP Dictionary):** hands-on training occurred, but no source artifact was saved to normalize or publish — see [Training Practices — Evidence Matrix](./training-practices/EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md).
- **`ZEMP_LOGALI` and `TEXT-001` dependencies** (labs 05, 06, 08, 04): documented, with an optional synthetic `_fq` replacement and setup steps in the [Runtime-Readiness Reference](./runtime-readiness/README.md) — not a requirement to close this track.

A status such as `NOT_FOUND_IN_THIS_LAB_SET` (see [Evidence Matrix](./EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md)) is local to this historical track. The broader portfolio already contains documentation-grounded RAP architecture, source-reviewed ABAP Unit design patterns and an executable non-live OData client-side example.

## Publication policy

This track excludes raw course files, certificate PDFs, QR/credential identifiers, credentials and private audit records. Published screenshots are limited to the account owner's own embedded captures, sanitized to remove account identifiers, tenant hostnames, technical usernames and training-environment data. This track's scope is complete on documented historical execution evidence and owner-confirmed hands-on training.
