# ABAP Evidence Sanitization Note

[Versión en español](./SANITIZATION_NOTE.es.md)

The public ABAP evidence set contains only completed training context, documented historical execution, public source, owned sanitized screenshots and current evidence boundaries. Raw course files, certificate artifacts, private identifiers and unexecuted reproducibility drafts are excluded.

Course 1 is represented as 16 curriculum units with 10 hands-on labs, 10 preserved `.abap` sources and 20 owned sanitized ADT screenshots. Course 2 is represented by completed hands-on DDIC training without fabricated source. Course 3 is represented by four owner-confirmed practices with normalized `_fq` source.
